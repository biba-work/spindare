## 🎮 How It Works (3 Steps)

### **1️⃣ Spin**
Tap the wheel and get a challenge personalized to your interests:
> *"Photograph something that reminds you of silence"*
> *"Ask a stranger what their favorite memory is"*
> *"Draw how you feel using only circles"*
> *"Find something broken and make it beautiful"*

Challenges come from **AI** (Gemini generates new ones every 5 minutes) or our **offline pool** (190+ curated challenges).

### **2️⃣ Complete & Post**
Take your challenge seriously:
* 📸 **Snap a photo**
* 🎥 **Record a video**
* ✍️ **Write your reflection**

Post it with an optional caption. Real people, real moments.

### **3️⃣ React & Connect**
See friends' posts appear live on your feed:
* React with **Felt**, **Thought**, or **Intrigued**
* Comment freely
* Send them a challenge back
* Start a **1-to-1 chat**

## ❓ FAQ

### **Is this really free?**
Yes, completely free. No ads, no premium tier, no microtransactions. We're figuring out monetization later.

### **Can I use it alone?**
Yes, but it's designed for friends. You can complete challenges solo, post, and build your own narrative.

### **What if I fail a challenge?**
You don't fail — you just show what happened. The app is judgment-free. Your real friends won't care; they'll find it funny.

### **Is my data safe?**
Yes. Clerk handles authentication securely, Supabase uses row-level security to protect your data, and everything is encrypted in transit.

### **Can I delete my account?**
Yes, anytime. Your data goes away, your posts are archived, and you're done.

### **Why no algorithm?**
Algorithms are designed to keep you addicted. We want you off your phone, not on it. We intentionally avoid recommendation engines.

### **Can I post without challenges?**
Not yet, but it's coming. For now, every post is tied to a challenge (that's the point!).

### **What about harassment or bullying?**
You can block anyone, report inappropriate posts, and we have moderation. But since it's just you and your real friends, bullying is rare.
